Privacy Policy

Effective: 14 September 2026

This policy explains how RANDA.MKCOOL Aim Sync handles information when you use the web, Android or iOS versions of the service.

Information we process

  • Account data: email address, account identifier and authentication/session information needed to create and secure your account.
  • Purchase and entitlement data: payment or store transaction references and entitlement status used to confirm access. Full payment-card details are not stored by RANDA.MKCOOL.
  • Configuration inputs: sensitivity, FOV, rotation mode, device profile, playstyle and gyroscope choices used to produce your requested Aim Sync output.
  • Operational/security data: limited request, error and security information needed to operate, protect and troubleshoot the service.

How information is used

  • Authenticate accounts and restore access.
  • Verify purchases and paid entitlements.
  • Generate requested sensitivity profiles.
  • Prevent abuse, protect the calculation service and investigate technical problems.
  • Provide customer support when you contact the business support address.

Service providers

RANDA.MKCOOL may use service providers such as Supabase for authentication/database/backend hosting, Stripe for eligible web payments, Google Play for Android store distribution and purchases, and Apple for App Store distribution and purchases. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

RANDA.MKCOOL does not sell personal information to advertisers.

Retention and deletion

Account and entitlement records are retained while needed to provide access, prevent fraud, meet legal/accounting obligations and resolve disputes. You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data that RANDA.MKCOOL is not legally required to retain.

Security

Authentication tokens are protected using platform-appropriate secure storage where supported. Sensitive backend credentials, signing keys and payment-provider secrets are not intentionally shipped in the public app source. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

RANDA.MKCOOL Aim Sync is a gaming utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children beyond what is necessary for an account and purchase flow. Store age-rating and family rules apply in each distribution platform.
