## Description

RANDA.MKCOOL Aim Sync is an unofficial Call of Duty: Mobile sensitivity and aim-setup utility built for players who want a consistent way to organise and test control settings.

Choose your base sensitivity, field of view, rotation mode, device profile, playstyle and gyroscope preference. Aim Sync returns a clear profile covering camera, firing and gyroscope categories for Multiplayer and Battle Royale.

FEATURES

• Multiplayer and Battle Royale sensitivity profiles
• Adjustable base sensitivity and FOV
• Fixed Speed, Speed Acceleration and Distance Acceleration rotation modes
• Camera, firing and gyroscope output categories
• Device, playstyle and gyro controls
• One-tap configuration copy
• Verified purchase restoration tied to your account
• Server-side calculation engine

Aim Sync is designed as a tuning companion. Generated values are starting points and do not guarantee game performance. Test adjustments in the in-game training environment before treating any setup as final.

RANDA.MKCOOL Aim Sync is unofficial and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or produced by Activision, Call of Duty, Tencent, or TiMi Studio Group.
