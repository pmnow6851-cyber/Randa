# AdMob setup — archived reference

Status: **not applicable to the current paid-only Aim Sync release**.

The canonical PWA and planned Android client use one £9.99 one-time unlock. They do not serve ads. Do not create AdMob app/ad units, add an ad SDK, or configure an ad payout profile as a release step.

Follow [the Android release gate](../docs/ANDROID-PLAY-RELEASE-GATE.md) and [the Flutter client README](README.md) for current signing, billing, privacy, deletion, and testing requirements. A future advertising model would require a separate owner-approved product change and a fresh policy and consent review.
