# RANDA.MKCOOL PRIVACY + MONETIZATION GUARDRAILS

Status: ACTIVE
Last aligned: 2026-09-07

## Purpose

Keep RANDA.MKCOOL operationally separate from the owner's private life while allowing the project to earn revenue through approved public channels without campaign spend.

## Non-negotiable privacy boundary

Public RANDA.MKCOOL assets must never expose:

- home address or postcode
- private email or recovery email
- private phone number
- bank account details or payout identifiers
- passwords, API keys, service-role keys, webhook secrets or tokens
- personal family, health, benefits, correspondence or unrelated private records
- private account recovery or identity information

Only the brand name, approved public app URL and approved public social identities belong in customer-facing content.

Any screenshot or media asset containing private information must be redacted or excluded before publication.

## Account separation

Use separate project identities for public-facing social, support, repository and marketing activity wherever the provider allows it.

Private recovery and payout information may be supplied directly to trusted providers when required for account security, KYC or payouts, but must not be copied into public source code, social captions, public profile metadata or campaign assets.

## £0 campaign spend

Campaign budget is **£0**.

Allowed:

- organic Facebook posts and Reels
- organic TikTok posts
- organic content on additional platforms once connected and verified
- free scheduling and analytics within existing plan limits
- free GitHub Pages hosting
- free/open-source production tools

Blocked unless the owner explicitly changes the rule:

- paid ads
- boosted/promoted posts
- paid social plans
- paid creator tools
- subscriptions or trials that can convert to charges
- paid influencer placements
- any automatic outbound marketing payment

## Revenue path

Preferred flow:

**Organic audience → official RANDA.MKCOOL Aim Sync app → approved checkout → payout provider → business payout account**

The system should minimize unnecessary outbound spending, but refunds, chargebacks, taxes, processor fees and statutory obligations remain subject to provider rules and law.

## Content privacy check before publication

Every post must pass all checks:

1. No private owner data visible.
2. No secret sensitivity formulas or internal coefficients visible.
3. No secrets, tokens, API keys or backend credentials visible.
4. Only strong/genuine gameplay proof is used.
5. Only the official app URL is promoted.
6. No unverified platform connection is claimed.
7. No paid boost or spend is enabled.
8. Product is described as unofficial and not affiliated with Activision, Call of Duty, Tencent or TiMi where appropriate.

## Social monetization strategy

Monetize in this order:

1. Convert organic social viewers into Aim Sync customers through the official app.
2. Grow followers, views, watch time and engagement on connected social platforms.
3. Apply for native platform creator monetization only when the account becomes eligible and when joining does not violate the £0-spend rule.
4. Keep all payout setup private inside the platform/provider dashboard.

Do not expose payout details publicly and do not claim platform monetization is active until eligibility and provider approval are confirmed.
